# Short-form video experts (roles, educators) and benchmark AI/tech accounts

Research date: 2026-10-12. Search coverage was thin: web search does not index Instagram/TikTok profile pages or individual Reels. Follower figures come from one third-party aggregator (CreatorDB), which is often internally inconsistent; treat them as estimates. Per-account format details (cover, first 3 seconds, length, voice) could NOT be verified from web sources and must be checked by watching the accounts directly (see Gaps).

## (a) Which roles own the cover, hook and retention chain, and which matter most for a faceless automated AI account

### Takeaway
No single "stylist" owns this. In the market the work splits into (1) idea + packaging (strategist / scriptwriter / hook writer, cover/thumbnail designer) and (2) execution (short-form or "retention" editor, motion designer). Job postings now merge these: the "retention editor" is expected to own the hook, pacing, retention curve and often the cover. For a faceless, automated AI-news account, the three critical roles are the hook/scriptwriter, the retention editor and the cover/thumbnail designer. The founder (or an automated pipeline) plays content strategist.

### Cited Findings
- Short-form/retention editor job postings focus on the opening seconds: editors who "hook viewers in the first seconds" and "engineer retention: the hook, the pacing, the watch-time curve." — [OnlineJobs.ph posting](https://www.onlinejobs.ph/jobseekers/job/1553430); [OnlineJobs.ph high-retention editor posting](https://www.onlinejobs.ph/jobseekers/job/1469592)
- Retention editor KPIs named in postings: Average View Duration / retention-curve stability, hook performance, analyzing retention graphs and adjusting the editing framework. — [OnlineJobs.ph high-retention editor](https://www.onlinejobs.ph/jobseekers/job/1469592)
- Editor postings often bundle thumbnail/cover design: "thumbnails that spark curiosity (not clickbait)", "thumbnail design eye" required. — [Dynasty Barber University posting](https://www.onlinejobs.ph/jobseekers/job/1544593); [Mediabistro Shorts Editor](https://www.mediabistro.com/jobs/3540226223-shorts-editor)
- Pay varies widely, from roughly $3/hour offshore to $2,800–3,200/month (small, non-representative sample of postings). — [OnlineJobs.ph](https://www.onlinejobs.ph/jobseekers/job/1553430); [SimplyHired short-form editor](https://www.simplyhired.mx/job/qL3Fy-XaPt01wfqkAiGHR7XwRlJW8jA05Yc1RNa1SkdVfLxqYQ886Q)
- Packaging (idea + title + thumbnail) is treated as a distinct pre-production specialty: Paddy Galloway, the best-known example, is described as a pre-production specialist in ideas, titles and thumbnails. — [Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/digital/teknologi/6a01ad4c0a759/mengenal-pekerjaan-ahli-strategi-youtube-di-balik-viralnya-youtuber-mrbeast); [Creator Science podcast](https://creatorscience.com/podcast/paddy-galloway-2/)

### Role map (role → what it does in the chain → Turkish term commonly used → importance for a faceless automated AI account)
The descriptions below are synthesized from the postings above plus general industry usage. They are not each individually sourced, so treat them as inference.
- **Content strategist / creative strategist** (İçerik stratejisti / kreatif stratejist): chooses which topics, series and angles to run, reads analytics and decides what to repeat. HIGH. In this project it is the founder plus the news-selection logic.
- **Hook writer / short-form scriptwriter** (Senarist, metin yazarı / "hook yazarı"; *copywriter* is also used): writes the first line, the on-screen headline and the script structure (curiosity loop, payoff). HIGHEST for a faceless account, because voice and text carry everything when no face appears on screen.
- **Short-form video editor / retention editor** (Kurgucu / video editörü; "Reels editörü"): sets cut rhythm, pattern interrupts, captions, B-roll timing and SFX, and owns the retention curve. HIGHEST. In an automated pipeline, these rules are encoded as templates.
- **Thumbnail / cover designer** (Kapak tasarımcısı / grafik tasarımcı): designs the Reel cover that appears in the profile grid and Explore. HIGH on Instagram, where the grid cover drives profile-to-follow conversion. It is less important for in-feed plays, which start on the first frame and not the cover.
- **Motion designer** (Motion tasarımcı / hareketli grafik tasarımcısı): animates text, UI demos and transitions. MEDIUM-HIGH for faceless accounts, which rely on kinetic typography and screen recordings.
- **Art director** (Sanat yönetmeni): owns the visual identity (palette, type, recurring layouts) so posts stay recognizable. MEDIUM. Usually a one-time setup that becomes a template.
- **Social media manager** (Sosyal medya yöneticisi / uzmanı): handles publishing, captions, hashtags, community replies, collabs and timing. MEDIUM.
- **UGC creator** (UGC içerik üreticisi): an on-camera person who makes native-looking content, usually for brands. LOW for a faceless account, though it is the classic way to make a product mention not look like an ad.
- Note: in Turkish, "stylist" (stilist) means fashion styling and is not a video role. The founder's "stylist?" question maps to art director or cover designer.

### Inferences
- For a one-person automated account, the practical "team" is a hook/script template, a retention-editing template and a cover template. Hiring a human retention editor or hook writer to audit the templates would likely give the most leverage per hour.
- Postings bundle hook, editing and cover into one person, which suggests the market sees these as one skill chain, not separate crafts.

### Gaps
- No authoritative Turkish job-market source was found for Turkish role titles. The Turkish terms above come from general usage and were not verified against Kariyer.net or LinkedIn TR postings.

## Well-known experts and educators, and what they are known for

### Takeaway
Paddy Galloway (packaging and ideas, MrBeast consultant) and Kallaway (short-form hooks) are the most relevant names. Instagram's own ranking statements (Mosseri) matter more than any guru for Instagram-specific behavior.

### Cited Findings
- **Paddy Galloway**: Irish YouTube strategist. Clients reportedly include MrBeast, Jesser, Ryan Trahan and Noah Kagan, and he is known for ideas, titles and thumbnails (pre-production). — [Creator Science guest page](https://podcast.creatorscience.com/paddy-galloway/); [Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/digital/teknologi/6a01ad4c0a759/mengenal-pekerjaan-ahli-strategi-youtube-di-balik-viralnya-youtuber-mrbeast). View-total claims (750M/month, 10B total) are unverified self-reports.
- Galloway principles (stated for long-form YouTube, not short-form):
  - "Core / Casual / New" test: an idea should appeal to fans, casual viewers and outsiders.
  - Thumbnail "glance test": it must be understood in under a second.
  - About 3 focal points maximum, with some empty space.
  - Slight exaggeration that does not mislead.
  - Time-box packaging to 1–2 hours.
  - Judge with early (first-hour or first-24-hour) CTR.
  - The episode explicitly does not cover short-form. — [Creator Science podcast](https://creatorscience.com/podcast/paddy-galloway-2/)
- **Kallaway (Kane Kallaway)**: short-form educator known for hook frameworks. A video covers "6 Hook Archetypes" and "4 Components of Winning Hooks", and he sells a hooks masterclass (2 hours, 37 pages of notes, 400+ hook database). Secondary summaries of his framework disagree, so the exact framework is unverified. — [Castmagic podcast page](https://www.castmagic.io/de/podcast/rhythm-hooks-and-a-billion-views-kane-kallaway-short-form-video-content-secrets); [skills.rest summary](https://skills.rest/skill/mkt-hook-kallaway)
- **Adam Mosseri / Instagram**:
  - In January 2025 he reportedly named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top ranking signals. Sends matter most for reaching non-followers. The often-quoted "sends weigh 3–5x likes" figure has no primary Mosseri source. — [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers); [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works) (secondary blogs)
  - Originality policy: since 2022, reposted Reels are deprioritized and aggregators are pushed down in recommendations; reposts were labeled in 2024. In April 2026 this was reportedly extended to photos and carousels: accounts that mainly share content they did not create or "meaningfully transform" are not recommended to non-followers. — [Tubefilter 2022](https://www.tubefilter.com/2022/04/21/instagram-adam-mosseri-algorithm-change-original-content/); [PetaPixel, 2026-04-30](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)
  - AI-profile labeling: Instagram renamed the "AI creator" label to "AI-generated profile" (around August/September 2026) and limits reach for AI-persona accounts that do not disclose. This concerns accounts whose *person* is AI-generated. — [Webrazzi 2026-09-01](https://webrazzi.com/2026/09/01/instagram-etiket-kullanmayan-yapay-zeka-hesaplarina-erisimi-kisitladi/); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/instagram-is-turning-down-the-reach-of-ai-influencers-who-hide-it)

### Inferences
- A faceless AI-news page that only re-posts others' clips is exactly what the 2026 originality policy targets. Original voiceover, own graphics and added analysis count as "meaningful transformation" and are needed for non-follower reach.
- Optimizing for *sends* (content people forward to a friend: "you need this tool") fits Instagram's stated signals better than optimizing for likes.

### Gaps
- Not researched within the call budget: Colin and Samir, Jay Clouse, Hormozi's short-form team, MrBeast's leaked production memo, and @creators official Reels guidance. These are known names, but no sourced claims about them were gathered here.
- No primary Mosseri video/post was fetched. The ranking-signal claims rely on secondary blogs.

## (b) Benchmark AI-tool / tech-news accounts (global and Turkish)

### Takeaway
Large global AI-news pages exist and are monetized through AI-tool sponsorships. Each is verified to exist via an aggregator, but their cover, hook, length and voice patterns could not be verified from the web. In Turkey, @jarrus.ai is the only verified dedicated AI-news Instagram page found, and it is carousel-first, not Reels-first.

### Cited Findings (global)
- **@evolving.ai**: about 4.83M Instagram followers (Oct 2026), about 38 posts/week, 3.5% engagement (category median 1.5%). Netherlands-based AI-news/AI-content media brand. Known partner: Higgsfield AI. Recent top videos got 0.6M–4.9M views. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/evolvingai/)
- **@theartificialintelligence**: about 1.34M followers, about 19.6 posts/week, 1.7% engagement. Short news-style posts on OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and agents. Has a newsletter, and no brand deals logged. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/theartificialintelligence/)
- **@theaipage**: about 579K followers, about 7.7 posts/week, 0.9% engagement, slightly declining. Bio "Make Time and Money with AI" (utility angle: tutorials, tool breakdowns). Sponsors: Higgsfield AI and Lovable. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/theaipage/)
- **@rowancheung** (founder of The Rundown AI): about 497K Instagram followers, 2.8% engagement. A face-on-camera, journalistic explainer of AI/robotics news. Partners: OpenArt and his own newsletter. — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/rowancheung/)
- Not verified (no source found): @airesearches, @aitoolsclub, @mattvidpro (known as a YouTube AI channel), Linus Tech Tips Shorts, MKBHD Shorts.

### Cited Findings (Turkish)

Its cover is a full-bleed striking image with a dark bottom gradient and a 3-line uppercase white headline, with no logo on the cover. — direct observation recorded in the project's memory note on 2026-10-11 (instagram.com/jarrus.ai). It was not findable via web search.

One low-quality career guide mentions "Tech Yapay" without data. — [RehberPanda](https://rehberpanda.com/blog/2026-sosyal-medya-influencer-icerik-ureticisi-youtuber-podcaster-komple-kariyer-rehberi-instagram-tiktok-youtube-monetizasyon-marka-isbirligi/) (unreliable)

### How these accounts present products without looking like ads (partial evidence)
- The common monetization is sponsorship by AI tools (Higgsfield, Lovable, OpenArt), which is consistent with tool demos being presented as "news/utility." — [CreatorDB evolvingai](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/evolvingai/); [CreatorDB theaipage](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/theaipage/)
- Galloway's "slightly exaggerate but don't mislead" packaging rule and editors' "curiosity, not clickbait" brief point to the line between engaging and ad-like/clickbait. — [Creator Science](https://creatorscience.com/podcast/paddy-galloway-2/); [OnlineJobs.ph](https://www.onlinejobs.ph/jobseekers/job/1544593)

### Inferences (patterns separating high-performing faceless AI-tool Reels from ad-looking ones; reasoned, not measured)
- **Lead with the outcome or news, not the brand.** "This free tool turns a photo into a 3D model" reads as news; opening on a logo plus feature list reads as an ad.
- **Show the real tool in use** (screen recording or result) within the first 1–3 seconds, so the product is the evidence, not the pitch.
- **Use a neutral, journalistic voice and attribute the source**, as @jarrus.ai does with source screenshots and credit lines and Rowan Cheung does with a journalistic style. Disclose paid collabs (the project's own no-prices + disclosure policy).
- **Make it worth a send:** a tool the viewer would forward to a friend. This matches Instagram's emphasis on sends per reach.
- **Keep it original:** own voiceover, analysis and graphics, not reposted demo clips, given Instagram's 2026 originality enforcement.
- **High volume is normal at the top:** @evolving.ai posts about 38 times a week and @theartificialintelligence about 20. Engagement rate does not scale with size (@theaipage at 0.9%), so the "utility/money" framing alone does not guarantee engagement.

Web search does not index Reels. These need manual viewing of about 10 recent Reels per account (logged-in browser).
