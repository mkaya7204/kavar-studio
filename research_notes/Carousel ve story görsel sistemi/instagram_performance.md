# Instagram Carousel & Story Performance (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-11. Data from 2025-2026 is prioritized, and older data is flagged. Many "algorithm" claims online come from vendor blogs, and each one is labeled as either official (Instagram/Mosseri) or third-party.

## 1. How the algorithm ranks carousels vs Reels vs single images, and recent changes

### Takeaway
Reels win on raw reach and discovery, especially for small accounts. Carousels win on engagement per reach, saves and views per post for established accounts. Single images are declining sharply. Instagram's own ranking docs say Feed and Explore rank on predicted time spent, likes, comments, shares/sends and saves, and Mosseri has emphasized sends per reach for unconnected (non-follower) reach. Recent changes: 20 slides per carousel (Aug 2024), 3:4 photo support (May 2025), a cap of 5 hashtags (Dec 2025), and originality rules extended to photos and carousels (Apr 2026).

### Cited Findings
- **Official ranking signals (2023, Mosseri, still the latest official explainer I found).** Feed predicts about 12 interactions, and the top 5 are: time spent on the post, commenting, liking, sharing, and tapping the profile photo. Explore weighs likes, saves and shares most, and how quickly a post gets engagement matters more there than in Feed. Stories are ranked on viewing history, engagement history (likes, DMs) and closeness, and the predicted actions are tap-into, reply and skip. Reels predict reshare, full watch, like and audio-page visit. Reels that are watermarked, low-res, bordered, majority-text or already posted on Instagram get less visibility. — [Instagram: Ranking Explained (May 2023)](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **Mosseri, Jan 2025 (secondhand).** The three most important signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends (DM shares) per reach. Distribution is split into connected reach (followers, where likes weigh more) and unconnected reach (non-followers, where sends weigh more). I found no primary Instagram URL for this. — [Dataslayer guide](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers); [MeetEdgar](https://meetedgar.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-explained-for-2025)
- **Socialinsider 2026 benchmark (35M posts, 447,613 pages, Jan-Dec 2025).** Engagement rate: carousels 0.55% (flat vs 2024), Reels 0.52% (was 0.50%), images 0.37% (was 0.45%). Overall rate is 0.48%, down 24% YoY. The page also lists Q1→Q2 2026 quarterly figures: Reels 0.50→0.48%, carousels 0.52→0.50%, images 0.35→0.33%. The page has internal inconsistencies: it is dated Feb 2026 but shows Q2 2026 data. — [Socialinsider Instagram benchmarks](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-research)
- **Socialinsider views per post (Reels / carousels / images), 2025.** By follower tier:
  - 1-5K: 580 / 993 / 417
  - 10-50K: 2,460 / 4,275 / 2,340
  - 100K-1M: 16,035 / 35,370 / 22,900

  Carousels lead views in every tier. — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-research)
- **Socialinsider saves per post (Reels / carousels / images).** By follower tier:
  - 10-50K: 7 / 8 / 3
  - 50-100K: 22 / 35 / 10
  - 100K-1M: 96 / 98 / 43

  Reels lead on comments (e.g., 10-50K: 12 Reels vs 10 carousels vs 4 images). — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-research)
- **Socialinsider engagement report (15M posts, Oct 2025-Mar 2026, medians per post, secondhand).** Reels: 33 comments / 5 shares / 35 saves. Carousels: 25 / 3 / 37. Images: 20 / 1 / 10. — [Contentdrips summary](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/)
- **Buffer "State of Social Media Engagement 2026" (52M+ posts, secondhand).** Engagement by reach is 6.90% for carousels vs 3.31% for Reels, but Reels get about 36% more reach. Buffer says Instagram "is functioning like two different platforms depending on which format you post." Other write-ups give a different metric: carousels have +114% engagement vs single images and +12% vs Reels. — [Contentdrips](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/); [Moneris](https://www.moneris.com/en/blog/posts/growth-strategy/why-you-need-carousels-for-your-social-posts)
- **Metricool 2026 Instagram Study (24.3M posts, 375K accounts, published 16 Jun 2026).** Carousels beat single images on every metric: 9x the saves, more than 2x the reach and views, and about 4x the interactions. Single-image reach fell 21.96% YoY and engagement fell 45.98% YoY. Accounts nonetheless post carousels 37% less often than single images. Instagram carousels got 4.7x the views of TikTok carousels. Average Reel watch time is 8.5s. — [Metricool press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/press-release/20260615-metricools-2026-instagram-study-reveals-reels-and-carousels-are-reshaping-1/)
- **Metricool + HypeAuditor (700M posts, Jan-Jun 2025, secondhand).** For accounts under 1K followers, median views are Reels 134, carousels 56, images 23, so Reels win discovery for small accounts. For brand accounts over 1M followers, carousels get 217,668 median views vs 110,500 for Reels. 93.5% of accounts post once a week or less. — [Contentdrips](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/)
- **20 slides.** The carousel limit was raised from 10 to 20 in August 2024. — [Online Tech Tips](https://www.online-tech-tips.com/?p=102378)
- **Music on carousels.** Music on carousel posts has rolled out globally. You pick a 5-90 second clip, which plays as people swipe. These are secondary reports, and I found no official date. — [Brandwatch](https://www.brandwatch.com/blog/react-post-a-slideshow-to-instagram/); [Decktopus](https://www.decktopus.com/blog/how-to-make-an-instagram-carousel-post)
- **"Second chance" re-serving.** Several blogs report that if a user scrolls past slide 1, Instagram may later re-show the carousel starting at slide 2 (some say slide 2 or 3). I found no official Instagram confirmation, and the behavior is widely repeated but undocumented. — [Postnitro](https://postnitro.ai/blog/post/increase-engagement-instagram-carousel-posts); [Contentdrips](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/)

### Inferences
- For a small or new Turkish tech account, Reels should drive follower acquisition, and carousels should drive saves, sends and dwell time from existing followers. Carousel views overtake Reels as accounts grow (Socialinsider tiers; Metricool's 1M+ data).
- Saves and sends are the metrics carousels win on, so carousel content should be "reference-worthy" (tool lists, comparisons, how-tos, prompts). That is a good fit for AI-tool and SaaS content.
- Plan for the unconfirmed slide-2 re-serve anyway: make slide 2 work as a standalone secondary hook. It costs nothing.

### Gaps
- I found no primary Instagram source for the Jan 2025 "sends per reach" statement. Treat it as widely reported.
- Meta's AI system cards for Feed, Stories and Explore were not retrieved in this pass.
- The original Buffer 2026 report was not accessed directly, and the figures come via summaries.

## 2. Benchmarks: ideal slide count, saves and shares rates

### Takeaway
Hard benchmark data on the optimal slide count is lacking. Practitioner guidance clusters at 8-10 slides, with fewer than 5 underperforming. Per-post saves and shares medians are listed above under question 1.

### Cited Findings
- Contentdrips recommends 8-10 slides, says fewer than 5 slides tends to underperform, and notes 20 are allowed. This is the author's guidance, with no study cited. — [Contentdrips (Jun 2026)](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/)
- Socialinsider gives no ideal slide count, only that carousels should be longer "when they add value". — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-research)
- Posting frequency, 2025 average posts per month (Socialinsider): Reels 8 (up from 6), carousels 5 (up from 4), images 7 (down from 10). — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-research)
- Audience growth rate fell in every tier from 2024 to 2025 (e.g., 10-50K: 33.8% → 17.2%). — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-research)
- One secondary blog cites a Socialinsider analysis of 150K posts: carousels get +55% reach and +70% saves vs single images. The date is unclear and I could not verify it. — [Bravery Technology](https://braverytechnology.com/instagram-algorithm-2026/)

### Inferences
- A default of 7-10 slides is reasonable for automated product carousels. More slides mean more dwell time and more re-serve opportunities, but only if each slide earns the swipe.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-2026 dataset that correlates slide count with reach or engagement. An older Socialinsider study existed, but it was not retrieved.
- I found no reliable per-format share-rate (shares / reach) benchmarks, only medians.

## 3. Hook slide, final-slide CTA and slide-2 importance

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous study on hook or CTA design. Evidence is indirect: Feed ranking predicts time spent, shares, saves and profile taps, and carousels are re-served from slide 2 (unofficial). Early engagement in the first 30-60 minutes matters.

### Cited Findings
- Feed predicts "time spent on the post (a few seconds)", comments, likes, shares and profile taps, so hook slides that stop the scroll and drive a swipe feed the time-spent signal. — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- Explore heavily weighs how quickly a post gets engagement. — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- Slide 2 may be served as the "cover" for users who scrolled past (unofficial). — [Contentdrips](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/)
- DM sends are claimed to carry 3-5x a like's algorithmic weight. This comes from CreatorFlow, is undated and is unverified. — [Contentdrips](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/)
- The first 30-60 minutes of engagement after posting influence distribution (vendor consensus). — [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/?p=14639)

### Inferences
- **Slide 1:** a bold claim or number with the Turkish keyword ("5 ücretsiz AI aracı…"), high contrast, minimal text.
- **Slide 2:** a second hook that works standalone.
- **Last slide:** one CTA. Given the weight on sends and saves, "Kaydet" (save) or "Bunu bir arkadaşına gönder" (send this to a friend) aligns with the ranking signals better than "follow". A comment-keyword CTA ("yorum: AI") generates comments plus DMs if paired with automation.

### Gaps
- I found no A/B data on CTA types (save vs share vs comment-keyword) for 2025-2026.

## 4. Captions, hashtags, keywords/SEO, alt text, posting frequency and time

### Takeaway
Hashtags are capped at 5 per post since Dec 2025, so keywords in captions, on-slide text and alt text now matter more, especially since public professional-account posts became Google-indexable in July 2025. Best times vary by source, so check Insights.

### Cited Findings
- On 18 Dec 2025, @creators (Threads) said Instagram "will gradually update the number of hashtags that you can include in a caption for a reel or post to five", down from 30. The cap reportedly covers the post as a whole, including comments. A French site claiming 3 is an outlier. — [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/12/19/instagram-puts-a-limit-on-hashtag-usage/); [Sirency](https://www.sirency.com/blog/instagram-hashtag-limit); [Multiplayer.it](https://multiplayer.it/notizie/instagram-limita-gli-hashtag-se-ne-potranno-utilizzare-massimo-5-per-post.html)
- Since ~10 Jul 2025, Google indexes public Instagram posts from professional accounts (18+). Captions, alt text and hashtags are reportedly indexed. There is an opt-out toggle, and sources disagree on whether Stories and Highlights are excluded. — [Search Engine World](https://www.searchengineworld.com/its-live-instagram-posts-are-ranking-and-showing-in-google-and-bing-seo-meets-social); [Pulse Advertising](https://www.pulse-advertising.com/news/instagram-content-now-google%E2%80%91indexable)
- Buffer advises adding keywords naturally to captions without stuffing, and other guides recommend custom alt text over auto-generated text. — [Buffer: Instagram SEO](https://buffer.com/library/instagram-search/)
- Sprout Social 2026 (≈2B engagements, 307K profiles, updated Mar 2026), best local times:
  - Monday 2-4pm
  - Tuesday 1-7pm
  - Wednesday 12-9pm
  - Thursday 12-2pm

  Tuesday and Wednesday are strongest, weekends weakest. — [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/best-times-to-post/instagram-media)
- Most accounts (93.5%) post once a week or less, which correlates with about 2% annual follower loss (Metricool/HypeAuditor 2025). — [Contentdrips](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/)

### Inferences
- For the automation: use 3-5 targeted hashtags (Turkish and English mix, e.g., #yapayzeka #aiaraçları #saas). Write Turkish keyword-rich captions where the first line repeats the hook, and auto-generate alt text per slide.
- Posting 2-3 carousels per week alongside Reels lands near the 2025 average of 5 carousels and 8 Reels per month.

### Gaps
- I found no Turkey-specific best-time data. Türkiye Gazetesi (2022, old) gives no concrete hours. Use Insights.
- I found no official guidance on optimal caption length for 2025-2026.

## 5. Stories: frames per day, tap-forward and exit, stickers, links, Highlights

### Takeaway
Exit rates are highest on the first frames (23.8% on frame 1) and decline after that. Frame 1 is the gate. Stories reach only a small share of followers for larger accounts. Tap-forward is about 57-67% for images and lower for video, and video performs better under 50K followers. Stories ranking depends on closeness and replies/DMs, so interactive stickers that generate replies fit the official signals, but I found no 2025 sticker benchmarks.

### Cited Findings
- **Socialinsider 2025 Stories benchmark sample.** 161,180 Stories from brand accounts, Jan-May 2025 vs 2024. — [Socialinsider Stories](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks)
- **Brand posting frequency by tier (Stories per month).** — [Socialinsider Stories](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks)
  - 1-5K followers: 12
  - 5-10K: 17
  - 10-50K: 35 (~1/day)
  - 50-100K: 50
  - 100K-1M: 80 (~3/day)
- **Exit rate by frame position.** Exit rates rose in 2025 vs 2024. — [Socialinsider Stories](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks)
  - Frame 1: 23.8%
  - Frame 2: 20.5%
  - Frame 3: 18.5%
  - Frame 4: 15.7%
  - Frame 9: 13.3%
  - Frame 15: ~12.5%
- **Tap-forward, image vs video.** — [Socialinsider Stories](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks)
  - 1-5K: 56.82% vs 50.50%
  - 10-50K: 63.70% vs 56.00%
  - 100K-1M: 66.00% vs 58.00%
- **Format.** Video outperforms images under 10K followers. 57% of brand stories are images and 43% video. — [Socialinsider Stories](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks)
- **Stories reach rate vs feed reach rate.** — [Socialinsider Stories](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks)
  - 1-5K: 3.3% vs 4.0%
  - 10-50K: 0.6% vs 3.7%
  - 100K-1M: 0.25% vs 2.5%
- **Reach and impressions by frame.** Reach rate rises with frame position (frame 1: 6.3%, frame 6: 20.5%, peak at frame 13: 37.8%), likely survivorship among committed viewers. — [Socialinsider Stories](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks)
- **Official Stories ranking.** Signals are viewing history, engagement history (likes, DMs) and closeness. Predicted actions are tap-into, reply and skip. — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **Stickers (old data).** In an Instagram Stories ads pilot, 9 of 10 poll-sticker campaigns increased 3-second views, and Dunkin' saw a 20% lower cost per video view. Instagram said about 60% of business Stories use an interactive element monthly. This is pre-2020 data. — [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/asos-dunkin-boost-engagement-with-poll-stickers-on-instagram-stories-ads/551380)

### Inferences
- **Story set structure:** 4-7 frames per topic.
  - Frame 1: strongest visual and hook, because it carries the highest exit.
  - Middle frames: a poll or quiz sticker, which generates the reply/tap signals that strengthen closeness for future ranking.
  - Final frame: link sticker plus a "Highlights'a eklendi" (added to Highlights) note.
- Keep text and stickers out of the top ~250px and bottom ~250-340px of 1080x1920 to avoid UI overlap. This is a common practice, not sourced in this pass.
- Small accounts should consider short video frames, or animated exports from the HyperFrames pipeline, over static images for Stories.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-2026 data on link sticker CTR, quiz/poll lift, or resharing feed posts to Stories.
- I found no data on Highlights' impact.
- Text safe-zone pixel values were not sourced.

## 6. Mixing carousels with Reels: does repurposing help or hurt?

### Takeaway
Originality rules target reposting other people's content, not repurposing your own. Repurposing your own content into a different format (Reel → carousel) is not penalized by any rule I found. Do avoid reposting the identical asset, watermarked exports from other apps, and bordered or majority-text Reels.

### Cited Findings
- **2024 rule.** Aggregator accounts that repost non-original content 10+ times in 30 days are removed from recommendations. — [Gigazine (May 2024)](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240502-instagram-updated-algorithm-prioritizes-original-content)
- **Apr 2026 rule.** Originality rules were extended to photos and carousels. Mosseri: "If most of what you post to Instagram is someone else's content, your account is no longer going to be recommendable." Followers still see the posts. Eligibility returns after about 30 days of mostly original posting. Adding commentary, using the native repost button or making collab posts counts, but borders, watermarks or caption credit do not. — [PetaPixel (30 Apr 2026)](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/instagrams-aggregator-purge-original-creators-gain-ground-as-repost-empires-crumble/)
- **Reels visibility.** Reels that are watermarked, bordered, majority-text or "already posted on Instagram" get reduced visibility. — [Instagram Ranking Explained (2023)](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **Original vs reposted.** Original content reportedly earns 40-60% more distribution than reposts (Net Influencer, Feb 2026, secondhand). — [Contentdrips](https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/)

### Inferences
- Turning a news Reel into a carousel with a different structure (deeper slides, comparison tables) is safe and probably beneficial, since it serves different audiences: Reels for discovery, carousels for saves. Use original renders, not screenshots of competitors' posts.
- Risk for an AI-tool news account: if most posts mainly repackage other companies' or creators' visuals (screenshots of others' posts), the April 2026 rule may apply. Add original analysis and design.
- Turning a Reel into a majority-text Reel is discouraged. Text-heavy content belongs in the carousel format.

### Gaps
- I found no official statement on posting the same topic as both a Reel and a carousel from the same account.

## 7. 1080x1350 (4:5) vs 1080x1440 (3:4)

### Takeaway
Since May 2025, Instagram supports native 3:4 (1080x1440) photos and carousels, and the profile grid uses 3:4 tiles, so 4:5 posts get side-cropped on the grid (centered ~1012x1350). However, the Meta Graph API's documented publishing range is reportedly still 4:5 to 1.91:1, so automated API publishing may reject or crop 3:4 files.

### Cited Findings
- **3:4 support.** Mosseri announced on Threads (~29-31 May 2025) that Instagram supports 3:4 photos for single posts and carousels. — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/05/29/instagram-finally-adds-support-for-34-aspect-ratio-photos); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/no-need-to-crop-your-photos-as-instagram-now-supports-34-aspect-ratio-for-photos-478651-2025-06-02)
- **Grid crop.** The grid shows a 3:4 center crop of 4:5 posts (about 1012x1350). Meta's API reference reportedly still lists 4:5 to 1.91:1, so schedulers using the API may crop 1440-tall files. — [Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/instagram-post-size/); [SocialBu](https://socialbu.com/blog/?p=20868)
- **Hopper HQ (Mar 2025).** Says grid tiles for posts show 4:5, with Reels covers at 1080x1440 on the grid. Its intro and body conflict. It recommends keeping key elements in a center safe zone. — [Hopper HQ](https://help.hopperhq.com/en/articles/10907941-instagram-s-vertical-grid)
- **Aspect ratio lock.** The first slide sets the aspect ratio for the whole carousel, so keep all slides identical. — [Postnitro docs](https://postnitro.ai/docs/how-to/set-carousel-size)

### Inferences
- **If publishing via the Graph API:** keep 1080x1350 (4:5). Place critical text inside the centered 1012x1350 band, about 34px margin left and right, so slide 1 also reads on the 3:4 grid.
- **If publishing manually in the app:** 1080x1440 avoids grid cropping and adds 6.7% more canvas. Test API acceptance before switching the pipeline.

### Gaps
- I did not verify current Meta Graph API content-publishing aspect-ratio limits directly. The developer docs should be checked before choosing 3:4.
- Turkey-specific data: Instagram is Turkey's most-used app with 62.3M users (Oct 2025), and average daily use is 1h53m (Minister Uraloğlu). I found no Turkey-specific format or time benchmarks. — [Teknoblog](https://www.teknoblog.com/instagram-turkiye-kullanici-sayisi/); [Bengütürk](https://www.benguturk.com/gundem/bakan-uraloglu-acikladi-turkiye-sosyal-medyadan-basini-kaldiramiyor-haftada-tam-264962h)
